A LETTER FROM MY HEART

Dear Chido...

♡
To my favourite person... ✿

Hey Sugar,

I have been thinking about a lot of things, and one thing I know is that I didn't want to let another day pass without finding a way to remind you how much you mean to me.

I know that things between us haven't always been easy. I know there are moments I could have handled differently, things I could have said better and times when I could have understood you more.

I am sorry for the moments when my actions or words may have made you feel otherwise. I don't want to dismiss your feelings or pretend that everything has been perfect.

I've realised that loving someone is about more than saying the right things. It's about showing up, listening, being patient, understanding and making someone feel valued through your actions.

I want to become better at those things. Not simply because I miss what we had, but because I recognise how important those things are.

I miss the little things. The conversations, the laughter, the random moments and the feeling of having someone so special to share life's ordinary days with.

I appreciate you more than I probably communicated sometimes, and I hope this little surprise reminds you of that.

With all my love, always. ♡

✧

If I am lucky enough to have another opportunity with you, I would love to approach it differently. With more patience, more understanding, more maturity, more intentional effort and a willingness to keep growing together.

I don't expect everything to change overnight, and I understand that trust and healing take time. I just want you to know that I am willing to put in the effort.

You are special to me chido. I hope you never doubt that.

Yours sincerely, with love. ♡
